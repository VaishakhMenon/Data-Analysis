## Key Investment Considerations

### ✅ What These Funds Offer
- **No lock-in period** - Redeem anytime (exit load may apply within 1 year)
- **Growth plans available** - Reinvest returns for compounding
- **SIP option** - Start with as low as ₹100-500/month
- **Regulated by SEBI** - High safety standards

### ⚠️ Important Points
- Exit load: Most funds charge 1% if redeemed within 1 year
- Taxation: LTCG above ₹1.25 lakh taxed at 12.5% (held >1 year)
- Minimum investment: ₹100-1000 for most funds
- Returns are not guaranteed - based on market performance

### 📊 Historical Performance Context
- Large Cap funds: 15-20% average over 5 years
