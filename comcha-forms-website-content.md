## 9. FAQ SECTION

### Section Header:
> **Frequently asked questions**

**Q: Is Comcha Forms really free?**
A: Yes! Our free plan includes unlimited forms and unlimited responses. No credit card required, no trial period. Free means free.

**Q: How is Comcha different from Typeform?**
A: We offer similar beautiful, conversational forms but with more generous free limits and more affordable paid plans. Get premium features without the premium price.

**Q: Can I use my own branding?**
A: On our Pro plan and above, you can add your logo, brand colors, custom fonts, and even use your own domain.

**Q: What integrations do you support?**
A: We integrate with Zapier, Google Sheets, Slack, Notion, HubSpot, Mailchimp, Salesforce, and 100+ other tools. If you use Zapier, you can connect to thousands more.

**Q: Is my data secure?**
A: Absolutely. We use SSL encryption, are GDPR compliant, and follow industry best practices for data security. Your data is stored securely and never sold to third parties.

**Q: Can I accept payments through forms?**
A: Yes! Connect your Stripe account on Pro plans and above to collect payments, donations, or subscriptions directly through your forms.

**Q: Do forms work on mobile?**
A: Every form is fully responsive and looks great on any device—desktop, tablet, or mobile.

**Q: Can I embed forms on my website?**
A: Yes! We provide embed codes for easy integration. You can embed as an inline form, popup, or slide-in.

**Q: What if I need help?**
A: We offer email support on all plans, with priority support for Pro users and dedicated support for Business and Enterprise customers.

**Q: Can I export my data?**
A: Yes, export all your responses to CSV or Excel anytime. Your data is yours.
